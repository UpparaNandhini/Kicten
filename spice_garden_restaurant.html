Our Story

Flavours Passed Down
Through Generations

Founded in 1998, Nandhu's Kitchen was born from a grandmother's recipe book and a passion for authentic Indian cooking. Every dish is crafted with hand-ground spices, traditional techniques, and a whole lot of love.

We believe food is a journey — and every plate we serve takes you deeper into India's diverse culinary landscape.
